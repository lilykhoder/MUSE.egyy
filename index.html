About MUSE

Founded in 2025, Muse was created to bring something different to the streetwear scene. We believe fashion should feel original, expressive, and unapologetically creative. Our goal is to introduce new styles and ideas that stand out from what’s already in the market — pieces that make people feel confident, inspired, and unique. At Muse, every collection is built around individuality, creativity, and pushing beyond the ordinary.
